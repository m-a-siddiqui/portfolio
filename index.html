The Cautio Command Center

The platform a 24×7 dashcam-monitoring floor runs on — allocation, attendance, footage requests and performance, in one system.

From spreadsheets nobody trusted to a single source of truth

Cautio · May 2025 – Present

Cautio builds AI dashcams for fleets. The cameras watch the road, but a person still has to watch the footage — so a floor of analysts reviews every client fleet, hour by hour, around the clock.

The problem

Around 410 client fleets needed eyes on them every day, each within its own contracted hours. Work was split by hand, tracked in spreadsheets and reviewed after the fact. Nobody could answer the three questions that mattered: who is covering what right now, what has gone unwatched today, and how do we know. Attendance was self-reported, and a night shift crossing midnight broke every total it touched.

What I built

A platform that allocates each hour's work across the floor weighted by fleet size, records attendance automatically, tracks footage requests across shift handovers, scores performance from figures anyone can check, and reconstructs any day on demand. It runs on the company's own Google Sheets — no migration, no new database — inside a hard 60-requests-per-minute quota shared by the whole floor.
